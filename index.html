Everything You Need to Manage Dairy

Mobile First Management

Run your entire dairy business directly from your mobile phone without needing a computer. Easily add and manage customers, record daily milk collection (morning & evening), update pricing, and monitor operations in real-time. The system is designed to work smoothly even on slow internet connections, ensuring uninterrupted usage in rural and semi-urban areas. Whether you are at the dairy, on delivery, or traveling, your business stays fully in your control at all times.

Automatic Billing System

Save hours of manual work with a fully automated billing system that calculates everything for you. Generate accurate monthly or custom-period bills based on milk quantity, fat rates, and pricing rules. Eliminate calculation errors, reduce disputes, and provide clear, professional invoices to your customers instantly. You can also maintain billing history, making it easy to track past records and ensure complete financial transparency.

Smart Payment Tracking

Stay on top of your finances with an intelligent payment tracking system. Monitor customer dues, partial payments, and full settlements with complete clarity. Get a detailed history of every transaction and avoid confusion in collections. This helps you improve cash flow, reduce outstanding balances, and maintain trust with your customers by keeping everything transparent and well-organized.

Customer Management

Manage all your customer information in one place with complete organization. Store details like name, phone number, address, milk type preference, delivery schedule, and billing history. Quickly access and update records anytime. This helps you provide better service, avoid mistakes in delivery, and build long-term relationships with your customers through consistent and reliable management.

Sales & Purchase Tracking

Track your daily milk sales and purchases with real-time insights. Maintain accurate records of how much milk is bought and sold each day, helping you understand your profit margins and stock flow. Analyze performance trends and make smarter business decisions with clear data. This ensures better control over your operations and helps you grow your dairy business efficiently.

Advanced Settings

Customize the entire system according to your business requirements. Easily configure milk pricing, fat calculation methods, packaging types, billing formats, and operational preferences. The flexible settings allow you to adapt the platform to your unique workflow instead of changing your process. This ensures maximum efficiency, accuracy, and convenience for your daily operations.
